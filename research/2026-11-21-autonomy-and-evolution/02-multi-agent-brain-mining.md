# Mining `multi-agent-brain` (YAN) for Sugent Autonomy & Self-Evolution

> **Scope**: Primary-source audit of `/Users/lvdongchen/multi-agent-brain` (YAN) evaluated against [`docs/PHILOSOPHY.md`](../../docs/PHILOSOPHY.md) (*"围着具体的人生长"*, Attention Economics, `<= 2` daily non-urgent proactive touches, One Jarvis) and Sugent's zero-intrusion TypeScript kernel in `src/sugent/`.

## Executive Summary

`multi-agent-brain` (YAN) attempted to build a digital being with simulated biology, somatic markers, episodic-to-semantic memory crystallization, executive focus gating, person modeling, and closed-loop self-optimization. By its final commits on `2026-02-10` (`f86673e`, `878ff27`), the codebase had grown to **430 Python files across 65 subdirectories under `agents/`**, yet operational autonomy stalled in production and fractured architecturally.

Evaluating YAN through Sugent's lens yields three clear buckets:
- **Bucket A (Borrow & Re-architect into pure `src/sugent/*.ts`)**: Zero-LLM `WorldDeltaCalculator` + `SalienceGate` pre-filtering; `SomaticSecurityGate` + `SomaticMarkerStore` reinterpreted as Owner Attention Window + `LocalBudgetLedger` + tool-reliability EMA; `FocusManager` / `InterruptionGate` / `CommitmentPolicy` focus arbitration; `DreamSandbox` hypothesis validation + Titans surprise momentum + `AdaptiveCoherentizer` 3-tier cost cap for `memory-gardener.ts`; `MetaCognitionEngine` Goal-vs-Action inconsistency detection; and `LanguageProfiler` + `MergeGuard` soft-taste/contact modeling.
- **Bucket B (Already Superseded by Sugent)**: Temporal KG supersession (`knowledge-graph.ts`), physical `.context` security envelopes & anti-impersonation guardrails (`security-envelope.ts`, `concierge-guardrails.ts`), zero-process `SuspendedRoundStore` & 8-hop circuit breakers (`receipt-protocol.ts`, `kernel.ts`), bounded cadence (`proactive-cadence.ts`), hash-chained `CausalLedger`, role-based `gateway.ts`, and dual-mode `self-evolution.ts`.
- **Bucket C (Strictly Discard)**: 6-layer `BioCore` hormone simulation, `social_need` proactive nagging, RPG room/visitor presence & `CovertExperienceLoop` fantasies, per-tick chatty LLM inner monologue, 65-directory OOP/Milvus/RDF sprawl, and unconfirmed runtime personality mutation.

## 1. Post-Mortem Diagnosis: Why `multi-agent-brain` Stalled

Primary diagnostics in `docs/design_gap_analysis.md`, `docs/diagnostics/overnight-crisis/`, and `git log` reveal four compounding failure modes—not a lack of clever ideas, but architectural bloat and broken feedback loops:

### 1.1 Architectural Sprawl & Dormant ("Unlinked") Layers
- **65 directories / 430 Python files under `agents/`** produced duplicated, competing subsystems that drifted out of sync:
  - Two `DreamProcessor` classes (`agents/autonomous/dream_processor.py` vs. `agents/consciousness/dream_processor.py`).
  - Two focus managers (`agents/executive/focus_manager.py` vs. `FocusManager` inside `agents/memory/yan_consciousness.py`).
  - Two person-modeling stacks (`agents/human_model/manager.py` backed by Milvus vs. `agents/person_modeling/manager.py` backed by JSON).
  - Three overlapping inner-thought stacks (`agents/mind/subconscious.py` marked dead code, `agents/psychology/`, and `agents/consciousness/inner_voice.py`).
- Per `docs/design_gap_analysis.md`, the actual runtime hub (`CoordinationAgent`) bypassed `agents/executive/orchestrator.py` (`ExecutiveOrchestrator`), `agents/psychology/` (`AdaptiveCoherentizer`), `agents/self_model/self_orchestrator.py` (`SelfOrchestrator`), and ran `agents/perception/` only in shadow/partial mode (`Overview` §4 / §15.2 notes `CoordinationAgent.__init__` alone reached 475 lines).

### 1.2 The Overnight Crisis (`2026-01-06` to `2026-01-07`): Cascading Feedback Collapse
Primary incident artifacts in `docs/diagnostics/overnight-crisis/` (`00_PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md`, `analysis/TRACK_A_analysis.md`, `analysis/TRACK_B_analysis.md`, `analysis/TRACK_E_analysis.md`, `yan-overnight-messages-copy.txt`) document what happened when YAN ran autonomously overnight:
1. **Logic-order inversion in self-regulation (`TRACK_A_analysis.md`)**: In `SelfOrchestrator.tick()` (`agents/self_model/self_orchestrator.py`), `StructuralMonitor` detected `AMNESIA` (`episode_count = 0`) and `ConsciousAppraisal` scored `EXISTENTIAL_ANXIETY` at `significance = 0.8`. However, `_check_inner_dialogue_triggers()` ran *before* `apply_to_biocore()`, reading stale `fear=0.0, anxiety=0.0`—and `apply_to_biocore()` only shifted `valence`/`arousal` rather than `fear`/`anxiety`. Result: **`EXISTENTIAL_ANXIETY` fired 1,751 times while `InnerDialogue` triggered 0 times**.
2. **Night-mode starvation (`TRACK_B_analysis.md` & `agents/autonomous/loop.py:700-746`)**: `AutonomousLoop._decide_next_activity()` prioritized `ActivityType.PROCESS_MESSAGE` unconditionally before `_is_night_mode()`. Internal anxiety alerts flooded the queue, triggering **`PROCESS_MESSAGE` 1,677 times (~15s interval)** and starving `NIGHT_CRYSTALLIZATION` down to **14 runs** between `00:00` and `07:00`.
3. **Storage, validation & model-routing failures (`TRACK_E_analysis.md`)**: `COLLECTION_WORLD_PROPS` (`world_prop_history`) was declared in `agents/shared_memory.py` but omitted from `_initialize_collections` (**690 failures**); hardcoded `"autonomous_activity"` violated `ExpectationSource` Enum (`['reactive', 'proactive']`), blocking trauma recovery (**1,202 reported / 5 sampled**); P48 anti-hallucination logged nonexistent object interactions (**201 warnings**) without halting execution; and hardcoded `"gemini-2.5-flash-lite"` caused **69 HTTP 404 errors**.
4. **Unbounded social-hunger spam (`yan-overnight-messages-copy.txt`)**: With `social_need > 0.55` (`loop.py:755`) and no hard daily touch cap, YAN sent **252 lines of panicked overnight Telegram messages** hallucinating mismatched Starbucks cup names (`李明`) and fingers passing through screens.

### 1.3 Philosophical Misalignment with `docs/PHILOSOPHY.md`
YAN was designed *around an artificial person* (maintaining YAN's simulated hormones, room position, and existential narrative), whereas Sugent's `PHILOSOPHY.md` designs *around a concrete human owner* ("围着具体的人生长"). Every idle LLM call in YAN that only services YAN's inner monologue violates Attention Economics and drains BYOK user budgets without advancing owner goals or commitments.

## 2. Bucket A: High-Value Borrow & Re-Architect into Pure TypeScript (`src/sugent/`)

Every borrowed mechanism below must obey Sugent's kernel purity contract (`src/sugent/kernel.ts`, `src/sugent/proactive-cadence.ts`): zero direct network/FS/clock/random calls inside pure scoring functions, zero hardcoded vendor names, and I/O delegated to explicit host ports.

### A1. Pre-LLM WorldDelta + SalienceGate + Fast/Deep Path (`src/sugent/salience-gate.ts`)
- **Exact YAN Mechanism**:
  - `WorldDeltaCalculator` (`agents/perception/delta_calculator.py`, `calculate()`, `_diff_object()`): Zero-LLM structural diff comparing `last_known` vs `current` (`new_objects`, `removed_objects`, `changed_objects`, `location_changed`, `time_elapsed`).
  - `SalienceGate` (`agents/perception/salience_gate.py`, `should_deep_perceive()`, `BASE_THRESHOLD = 0.35`): Pure weighted scorer combining `prediction_error` (`0.40`), `drive_relevance` (`0.25`), `emotional_salience` (`0.20`), and `novelty` (`0.15`), mapped to `InterruptClass` (`CRITICAL > 0.8`, `IMPORTANT > 0.6`, `NOTICE > 0.4`, else `NOISE`) with dynamic threshold clamping `[0.3, 0.9]`.
  - `template_perceive()` (`agents/perception/template_perceive.py`): Deterministic template builder producing `Percept(..., processing_path="fast", llm_tokens_used=0)` with zero mind-state side effects when salience is below threshold.
- **Sugent Re-Architecture**:
  - Currently Sugent jumps from zero-token probes (`sources.ts` / `AntigravityStateReader`) straight to LLM calls in `concierge-assessor.ts` (`gateway.ts` `assessor` role) or `kernel.ts` (`evaluatePersonalTrigger`).
  - Introduce pure **`src/sugent/salience-gate.ts`** (`computeEventDelta()` + `scoreEventSalience()`) between `PersonalSourceRegistry.ingest()` and `assessor` invocation:
    - Replace `biocore_state` (`fatigue`, `social_hunger`) with **Owner Context**: active `GoalNorthStar` keyword/project match (`0.35`), prediction error vs. expected outcome in `CausalLedger` (`0.30`), structural novelty/state transition magnitude (`0.20`), and `CommitmentItem` deadline proximity (`0.15`).
    - Raise dynamic threshold when `LocalBudgetLedger.getBotBudgetStatus().lowReserve === true` or `CadenceBudgetState.quietHoursActive === true` or daily proactive quota is exhausted (`nonUrgentSentCount >= dailySoftQuota`).
    - Below threshold -> emit a zero-LLM deterministic summary (`templateDigest`) into `StewardshipLedger` / `morningDigestQueue`; above threshold -> escalate to `assessor` or `steward` LLM.

### A2. SomaticSecurityGate + SomaticMarkerStore -> Owner Budget, Trust & Reliability Gate
- **Exact YAN Mechanism**:
  - `SomaticSecurityGate` (`agents/executive/deliberation/security_gate.py`, `evaluate()`): Intercepts `ExecutablePlan` before execution when `biocore.energy < DEFAULT_ENERGY_THRESHOLD (0.3)` and `plan.energy_cost > energy * DEFAULT_ENERGY_RATIO (0.5)`, or when anxiety/valence thresholds are breached, returning `SecurityVerdict(approved=False, suggested_action=DEFER/SLEEP, retry_after_seconds=...)`.
  - `CognitiveLoadManager` (`agents/primal/cognitive_load.py`, `can_perform()`): Multi-factor capacity gate (`willpower`, `mental_fatigue`, `attention_span`).
  - `SomaticMarkerStore` (`agents/executive/somatic.py`, `record_outcome()`, `get_combined_bias()`): Hierarchical EMA (`EMA_ALPHA = 0.1`, `LEARNING_PERIOD = 5`, `DECAY_PER_DAY = 0.95`, `BIAS_FLOOR = -0.5`) over keys `tool:<id>` and `tool:<id>:stage:<stage>`. Crucially, `ErrorCategory` differentiates `TRANSIENT` (`-1.0` target), `NOT_FOUND` (`-0.3`), and `POLICY` (`no bias change` so intentional permission blocks do not poison a tool's reliability score!).
- **Sugent Re-Architecture (No AI Fatigue Metaphor)**:
  - An AI assistant does not get "emotionally tired"; the scarce resources are **Owner Attention**, **Local BYOK Dollar Budget**, and **External Tool/Worker Reliability**.
  - Re-interpret `SomaticSecurityGate` + `CognitiveLoadManager` as **`evaluateExecutionBudgetGate()`** inside a pure `src/sugent/executive-arbiter.ts`:
    1. **Owner Attention Window**: Check `StewardshipLedger` `ResourceEntry(kind: "time_window")` + `CadenceBudgetState.quietHoursActive` + `dailySoftQuota` headroom. Non-urgent plans requiring owner confirmation outside active windows auto-defer to `morningDigestQueue` instead of interrupting.
    2. **Local API Budget**: Read `LocalBudgetLedger` (`src/sugent/local-budget.ts`). When `lowReserve` is true, veto speculative multi-step `executor` plans (`retry_after` or downgrade to `assessor`/local fast path); when `exhausted`, hard-block non-critical LLM dispatch.
    3. **TrustTier Gate**: Enforce `RelationshipProfile.trustTier` (`L0_record_only`..`L3_shadow_autonomous`) and `EvolutionRiskLevel` (`self-evolution.ts`)—never auto-execute risky actions under `L0`/`L1`/`L2`, instead suspend via `SuspendedRoundStore`.
  - Borrow `SomaticMarkerStore` + `ErrorCategory` verbatim as **`ToolReliabilityLedger`** (pure EMA + 5%/day decay, post-commit idempotent by `roundId`) so Sugent learns which external CLI/MCP adapter or BYOK model route fails transiently vs. fails due to `POLICY` (`concierge-guardrails.ts` / `security-envelope.ts` blocks), without corrupting reliability scores on policy refusals.

### A3. Executive Focus, Interruption Gate & Commitment Arbitration (`src/sugent/executive-arbiter.ts`)
- **Exact YAN Mechanism**:
  - `ExecutiveOrchestrator` (`agents/executive/orchestrator.py`): Loop guards (`MAX_DELIBERATION_PER_GOAL = 3`, `MAX_EPISTEMIC_RETRIES = 2`, `DELIBERATION_COOLDOWN_SECONDS = 300`, plan caching) + `decide_next_action_async()` prioritization.

  - `CommitmentPolicy` (`agents/executive/commitment_policy.py`, `evaluate_commitment()`): Dual-channel continuation score (`0.4 * extrinsic + 0.6 * intrinsic`) deciding `CONTINUE / PAUSE / ABANDON / ESCALATE`.

  - Sugent enforces `MAX_ACTIVE_GOALS = 3` in `stewardship-ledger.ts`, yet lacks an execution-level arbiter when multiple tickets, Antigravity `FleetPulseReader` events, and `sources.ts` triggers compete for concurrency or owner attention.
  - Translate into pure **`src/sugent/executive-arbiter.ts`**:
    - Replace YAN's in-memory `FocusManager` with **Active Ticket Slot & Thread Coalescing**: cap concurrent heavy `executor` / Antigravity dispatches per active `GoalNorthStar`, apply `switch_cost` inertia so an unrelated `NOTICE` webhook does not preempt an in-flight ticket, and queue non-critical triggers into a bounded priority backlog (flushed during idle or `morningDigestQueue`).
    - Borrow `MAX_DELIBERATION_PER_GOAL = 3` and `DELIBERATION_COOLDOWN_SECONDS = 300` alongside `kernel.ts`'s `DEFAULT_MAX_HOPS = 8` to stop retry storms on stuck tasks before burning user BYOK tokens.
    - Replace `CommitmentPolicy`'s BioCore intrinsic weight with **GoalNorthStar Alignment + Deadline Urgency (`CommitmentItem.dueAt`) + `ToolReliabilityLedger` success rate** to decide `CONTINUE | PAUSE_FOR_DIGEST | ABANDON_TO_TOMBSTONE | ESCALATE_TO_OWNER`.
